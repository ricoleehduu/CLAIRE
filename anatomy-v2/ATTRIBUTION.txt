STS24/25 remain under their respective source dataset terms; no ToothFairy3 license is applied to them.
PROCESSING: 2-voxel sampling; Gaussian sigma 0.45 sampled voxels; marching cubes at 0.5, step 2 for jaw/sinus/airway. Full NIfTI affine applied after canonicalization, then world coordinates converted to Y-up. ToothFairy3 selected files have reversed superior/inferior anatomy relative to their header; a 180-degree rigid rotation about X corrects this, verified by upper/lower FDI tooth labels. STS24/25 retain their already-correct vertical orientation; STS25 orientation checked against matching FDI masks. Surfaces are centered. These are visualization derivatives, not unmodified voxel GT or quantitative measurement tools. Dataset identities do not denote independent clinical centers. No private patient identifiers, imaging volumes or prediction masks are bundled.
