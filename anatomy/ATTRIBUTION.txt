Derived by CLAIRE: label maps sampled at 2-voxel intervals, Gaussian sigma 0.55 sampled voxels, marching cubes at 0.5; jaws and other structures use marching-cubes step 2. RAS to glTF Y-up; centered per case. Visualization surfaces are not original voxel annotations and are not suitable for quantitative measurement. No prediction or manual anatomy creation. First four filename-sorted cases in each public acquisition subset A/B/C; these are not hospital-center identifiers.
